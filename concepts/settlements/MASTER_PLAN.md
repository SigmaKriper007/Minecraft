# Settlements master plan

Updated: 2026-08-26.

## Design rules

- Survivor residents use only original project-owned skins. Real creator likenesses and downloaded skins are excluded.
- Survivor settlements are rare Overworld landmarks built as one terrain-adapted village template with guaranteed persistent residents.
- Residents have player-scale health and movement, trade expedition compasses, equip useful dropped gear, and retain equipped gear across reloads.
- Residents are neutral to players until attacked. Below three hearts they retreat instead of fighting.
- Residents avoid Haiku while unarmed; an Opus-tagged weapon changes that relation into active defense.
- Passive armor-set abilities are available to resident AI. Player-input abilities are translated into bounded AI actions where applicable.
- The later Japanese settlement is a separate culture, structure and combat branch; it does not reuse survivor skins.

## Delivery order

1. Survivor settlement, residents, trades, equipment and Haiku relations.
2. Japanese settlement landscape, ninja/samurai roster and katana progression.
3. Young Samurai encounter, loot and settlement integration.

## Japanese branch lock

- The `japane.jpg` reference informs the broad white-wall/dark-timber castle, layered sweeping roofs and raised balcony silhouette.
- A shallow generated river crosses the settlement approach beneath a curved timber bridge; cherry trees frame the outer paths and do not replace the arena.
- Black Ninja are fast territorial skirmishers with ordinary steel katana and smoke-step attacks.
- Samurai are `1.3x` player scale, tougher and slower, wielding long katana with a committed lunge.
- The central court contains exactly one persistent Young Samurai: a reference-led Bloodflower duelist with five sword/taijutsu techniques, two phases and teleport evasion.
